fractallambda

Tools

Small things that do one job. Each runs entirely in your browser — no accounts, no servers, nothing sent anywhere.

  • Ranker Turn any list into an ordered one by comparing two things at a time. Answer “which do you prefer?” until the order falls out. →
  • Decision matrix Score options against the things you care about — having weighed those by comparison rather than guessing the percentages. →
  • Calibration trainer Find out whether your “90% sure” is actually 90%. Give a range rather than a number, and see how often the answer is inside. →
  • Cron explainer Read a cron expression in plain English, and see exactly when it fires next — in the time zone the daemon actually runs in. →
